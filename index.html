Step 1: Select Material Specimen
STEP 01 Material Prep

Select Test Specimen

Choose from engineering materials with distinct ductile or brittle failure behaviors.

Mild Steel (AISI 1018): Ductile carbon steel with pronounced barrelling under compression without brittle fracture.
STEP 02 Surface Prep

Clean Machine Platens

Degrease and wipe the hardened compression platens to eliminate abrasive grit.

STEP 03 Metrology

Measure Initial Dimensions

Use the 0.02 mm virtual Vernier Caliper to measure initial height (h₀) and diameter (d₀).

STEP 04 Centering

Mount Specimen on Lower Platen

Align cylinder on concentric alignment grooves to eliminate eccentric bending.

STEP 05 Seating

Seat Upper Compression Platen

Lower the upper crosshead until the spherical platen touches the top face (zero clearance).

STEP 06 Safety Protocol

Supervisor Safety Green-Light

Slide blast shield closed and obtain Supervisor digital verification to unlock hydraulic pump.

STEP 07 Calibration

Zero Load Cell & Dial Gauge

Tare digital readouts and reset analog dial peak follower needle.

STEP 08 Active Test

Apply Compressive Load

Enter compressive load (kN) manually or run automated continuous hydraulic testing.
